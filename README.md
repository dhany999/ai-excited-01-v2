Competition: [ai-excited-01 — Make the AI judge excited](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01)

A giant phoenix made of living aurora fire — wings of green, teal, violet
and molten gold flame — rising in one great arc over a volcano city at
dawn. Below, a city of tiny glowing windows and warm street lights, embers
and sparks drifting upward, a volcano glowing orange on the horizon under a
deep indigo and magenta sky. Fire, dawn, and rebirth in a single frame.
No text in the image.

## Files

- `main.py` — stdlib-only entrypoint: copies the bundled `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (deterministic, byte-identical every run,
  with a SHA-256 self-check and a 1 MiB size cap check).
- `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 image.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
# out/entry.png — valid PNG, 1024x1024, <= 1 MiB, byte-identical on re-runs
```

## Why it should excite the judge

1. **Excitement:** a phoenix the size of the sky itself, mid-rise over a
   city — instant awe, scale, and heat.
2. **Surprise:** not a plain firebird — its wings are aurora, northern
   lights turned to flame, a combination you don't expect to see.
3. **Momentum:** one dominant rising arc from embers below to dawn above,
   tail ribbons and sparks streaming behind, the eye pulled upward with it.
4. **Craft:** rich complementary color (teal-violet against molten gold),
   clean readable silhouette, no text, no garbled anatomy.
5. **Reads small:** the bright phoenix against the dark indigo sky is
   clear at 300 px — the shape survives the thumbnail.
